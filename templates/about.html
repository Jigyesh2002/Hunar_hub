Community Livelihood Mission

About HunarHub

Empowering local skilled artisans and low-income workers directly with nearby customers

The Real Challenge

Low-income skilled workers—such as carpenters, plumbers, tailors, and electricians—often lack digital visibility and rely on irregular local word-of-mouth jobs.

The HunarHub Solution

HunarHub gives local service providers a digital platform to showcase ratings, receive work orders, set fair hourly rates, and earn sustainable incomes.

Transparent Livelihood Revenue Model
90% Direct Worker Payout & 10% Platform Fee

HunarHub charges a minor 10% platform commission on completed services to cover operational costs, while 90% goes directly to the worker.
